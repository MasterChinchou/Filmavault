About Filmavault

Filmavault is a functioning internal database website for cinema staff of FilmaVault. Its a website that stores information about films, directors, cinemas, viewers, screenings, and ticket bookings in one main organised database. This makes it easier for users to navigate and check out what they need.

The staff can use it to quickly look up which films are screening, where, and when, and to check viewer booking details. Cinema managers can use it to see a much higher level of information such as the most popular films and the most busiest cinemas. information which are really helpful for managing.

The site is designed to be simple and easy to navigate, showing only the information staff need so it stays quick and easy to use.
